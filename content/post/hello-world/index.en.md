Welcome to my digital garden — really just a place to log my learning.

This is the first post after the rebuild. As tradition goes: why I rebuilt the blog, and what I plan to write here.

## Why rebuild

I used to run WordPress. Writing was fine, but at some point it stopped fitting:

- Tweaking the design meant fighting the theme;
- Adding a small feature meant stacking plugins, one after another;
- The server needed renewing — and forgetting once meant everything went dark.

So this time I moved to Hugo with the open-source Stack theme: posts are plain Markdown files, versioned in a repo. The theme handles the layout, and I only tweak the small bits. Much less noise while writing.

> Old posts will be migrated here gradually. No rush — one at a time.

## What I'll write

No strict plan, but roughly:

1. **Study notes** — things I sort out after courses, books, or docs;
2. **CTF & security** — fun challenges and lessons learned while practicing;
3. **Tinkering logs** — Linux, self-hosting, tools, breaking things and fixing them.

Nothing too advanced; I'm still learning. But one promise: **everything here was tried with my own hands.** Writing it down first helps my future self.

## About this site

The stack, for the curious:

- Framework: Hugo
- Theme: Stack
- Hosting: GitHub Pages

Let's get started. 🌱
